Cave Dive

Somewhere at the end of the line there is a pocket of air. Get there with your hands.

✋Hold the left or right mouse button to reach out with that hand and grip the rock you are looking at.
⬇️While gripping, pull the mouse back to drag yourself forward. Then grab with the other hand. Left, right, left, right.
🌊Grab at open water and you do a weak swim stroke instead.
🟫Grab the floor and you stir up silt. Then you can't see a thing for a while.
🧵Follow the white guideline. It leads to the air.
⌨️Keyboard: Q / E grab, W pull, arrows look. Phone: hold the hand buttons and drag down.
