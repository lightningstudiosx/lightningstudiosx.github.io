How to play

You are the sky

Lumi walks across the valley on their own and never sees you. They stop when the way is blocked: deep water, a gap, fire, fog, a shut gate.

Pick a brush and drag across the sky. Rain fills ponds, grows vines and puts out fires. Too much floods the path.

Sun melts ice and snow, dries floods and mud, burns off fog and ripens crops. Too much makes Lumi thirsty.

Wind: draw a stroke the way it should blow. It pushes clouds, boats, windmills, leaves and fog.

Snow freezes water into ice you can walk on. Lightning is a one-shot tap: it knocks down dead trees and lights beacons.

Every stroke spends paint. Stars: arrive, stay under par, keep Lumi happy. Lumi never gets hurt: they wait, get soggy, or paddle back to the campfire.

Keys: 1–5 brushes · R restart · P pause · F fast-forward · H hint · M mute
