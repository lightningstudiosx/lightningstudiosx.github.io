HOW IT WORKS
  1. START CAMERA and allow webcam access
  2. hold your hand up — a green dot follows your index finger
  3. move the dot over one of your pieces
  4. pinch thumb + index together to GRAB it 🤏
  5. keep pinching and drag to a green/red square
  6. release the pinch to drop the piece
  7. drop anywhere illegal and it snaps back home
works instantly — no calibration, your hand IS the mouse. camera is processed entirely in your browser (mediapipe), nothing is uploaded. the mouse also still works if your arm gets tired.
