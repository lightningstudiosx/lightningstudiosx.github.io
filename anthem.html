About the tune

The melody was written just for Kaiya. It is in C major, in 4/4 time, and every line of words takes two bars. The chorus climbs higher than the verses so it feels like a big shout of joy, and the last line of the song jumps up to the highest note of all — on "stars".

The band is a tiny synthesizer that lives in your browser: a melody voice, soft chords, a bass line and a little drum kit.
