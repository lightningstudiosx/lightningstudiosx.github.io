THE MOVER'S HANDBOOK

1 · Pack

  • Drag things from the curb into the truck. They fall until something holds them up, so build from the floor. Tap a thing to select it, then tap the truck to drop it there.
  • R or Rotate turns what you're holding; F flips it. Liquids, pets and THIS SIDE UP boxes cannot be turned.
  • Fragile things (glass icon) break if something heavy (3 or 4 weights) sits on them. Bubble wrap saves a fragile thing once.
  • Pets need at least 2 empty squares beside their carrier, and nothing on top. Pets never get left behind.
  • Floating things rise instead of fall. Invisible things still take up space.
  • Not everything has to fit, but the client counts what's left on the curb.

2 · Drive

  • Hold → / GAS to accelerate and ← / BRAKE to slow down. Pedals press in gradually, so tap them for gentle driving.
  • Flooring it throws loose cargo backwards; slamming the brakes throws it towards the cab. Loose things slide until they hit something, and slamming into things breaks fragile stuff.
  • Bumps, potholes and hill crests bounce loose things up. Anything that drops 2 squares lands hard. Heavy things that land on fragile things crush them.
  • Straps hold a thing completely still. Tall tippy things fall over unless strapped or braced by a neighbour. Liquids slosh on every jolt and spill after 3 sloshes.
  • Stop fully at stop signs. Take roundabouts under 25 km/h. Park in the yellow driveway at the end.

3 · Get paid

  • The client pays for what arrives intact, adds a bonus for a quick drive, and writes a review. Good reviews earn stars; stars unlock gear in the Garage and the next move.
  • Everything you break goes to the Museum of Broken Things, with a plaque.
