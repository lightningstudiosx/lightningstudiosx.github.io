How to build

  • Place: pick a part, click the board. R turns it.
  • Wire: click a gold output pad (with a ring), then an input pad. Traces glow when they carry a 1.
  • Select: flip switches, hold buttons, drag parts, click an LED to change its color. A selected gate shows its truth table.
  • Delete: click a part or a trace. Del removes the selected part, Ctrl+D copies it.
  • Undo / redo: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
  • Flip-flops and the counter change on the rising edge of CLK. The 7-segment display shows its 4 inputs as a hex digit.
  • Camera: right-drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Two fingers on touch.
  • The buzzer makes sound once you turn Sound on.
