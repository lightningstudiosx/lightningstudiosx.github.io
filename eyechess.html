HOW IT WORKS
  1. START CAMERA and allow webcam access
  2. CALIBRATE: look at each dot and click it — one per square (or quick 9-dot)
  3. on your turn: look at your piece
  4. blink to select it (or press SPACE)
  5. look at the destination square
  6. blink again to move
  7. blink on the same piece to deselect
your camera is processed entirely in your browser by webgazer.js — no video ever leaves your computer. calibration is saved for next time. blink detection is imperfect: SPACE always works, and dwell mode (stare 1.2s = blink) is in the buttons.
