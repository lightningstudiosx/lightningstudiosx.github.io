PIZZA
PHYSICS
Toss it. Top it. Deliver it. Don't drop a single olive.
0 🪙
1. Toss - flick the dough up and catch it under your hand. Every catch stretches it bigger.
2. Top - drag toppings onto the pizza. They are real: olives roll, anchovies slide, cheese sticks.
3. Deliver - gas, brake and honk your way to the customer. Every bump shakes the tower!
