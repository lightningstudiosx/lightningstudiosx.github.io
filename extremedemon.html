I'M THINKING OF ANEXTREME DEMON

20 Questions meets Guess Who, for every rated Extreme Demon. Ask yes/no questions, cross demons off the board, name it in as few questions as you can.

How to play

The Thinker secretly picks an Extreme Demon from the pool. The Guesser asks yes/no questions: type them, or build one with the question builder ("Is it in the top 50?", "Was it verified by ...?", "Is it a 2.2 level?").

The Thinker answers YES, NO, SORT OF or DON'T KNOW. Use the board to ✕ cross out demons that can't be it and ★ star the suspects. When you're sure, make a 🎯 formal guess. A wrong guess costs a life.

Vs Bot and the Daily also take questions in plain words ("is it a wave level?", "what's the theme?"). Facts are answered from the data; turn on the optional AI answers (a small model that runs on your device) for open questions. It is never allowed to say the name.

Online, the Thinker's pick is locked with a hash before the first question and revealed at the end, so you can check nobody switched demons.

Scoring: a solved round is worth 1000, minus 50 per question and 200 per wrong guess (at least 100). If the Guesser runs out of lives, the Thinker scores 500.
