    What it can’t do (yet)

    • It only knows the ~225 words in the dictionary. Anything else stays in [brackets].
    • It translates sentence by sentence using rules — no idioms, no jokes, no “raining cats and dogs”.
    • Long sentences with “who / which / that …” clauses, and passive sentences (“the cake was eaten”) come out rough.
    • English words with two meanings get its best guess (“fly” the bug or “fly” the verb).
    • Kaihana has one word for he, she and it (ya), so going back to English says “he/she”.

    Numbers

    Count like you say it: tens, then ones. kaito = 10, honu = 100, salu = 1000.
