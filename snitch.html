📍 Where are you?

🌐 What's your IP address?

Your IP is the address websites send things back to. This one needs an outside website (ipify.org) to tell us, so pressing it sends your IP to them. That's the same as visiting any website.

🛡️ How to snitch less
  • Use a privacy browser like Firefox (with Strict tracking protection), Brave, or Tor Browser. They lie about or hide a lot of this.
  • Say no to permission pop-ups (location, camera, microphone, notifications) unless you really need them.
  • Install fewer weird fonts and extensions. Unusual setups make you easier to tell apart.
  • A VPN hides your IP address, but not your fingerprint. Everything else here still shows.
  • Private or incognito mode forgets cookies and history, but your fingerprint stays the same.
