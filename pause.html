The Game of Pausing

this game is always static. every single frame is pure noise — screenshot any of them and there is nothing there. but while it moves, you can see everything: the road, the walls, the gems, you. that's the trick your eyes do with moving noise, and it's the only trick this game has.

pause it, screenshot it, tab away, press Escape: the frame freezes, and a frozen frame is just… static.

you roll forward and keep getting faster · A / D or ← → to steer · SPACE to jump · dodge the walls · collect the gems (they hum)
