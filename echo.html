ECHO

You are in a cave with no light at all. The only way to see is to make a sound.
Something else down here is listening too.

👏Space or click to clap. The sound bounces off the walls and you see them for a moment.
🕹️WASD / arrows to walk (on a phone: drag, and the CLAP button). Footsteps make small sounds.
🤫Hold Shift to sneak. Silent, but you can't see a thing.
👂The creatures are blind. They hunt sound. A clap wakes everything nearby.
💧The exit drips. Follow the green ripples.

Headphones make it better: you can hear which side things are on.
