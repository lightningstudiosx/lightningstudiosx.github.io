ECHO

A cave with no light at all. All you have is a sound scanner.
Everywhere the sound hits, you see a dot. Something else down here is listening.

📡Hold the mouse to scan where you are looking. The battery drains while you scan and refills when you stop.
👏Space to clap: everything around you lights up at once. You only get a few claps per cave.
🕹️WASD to walk, mouse to look (click first). Shift to sneak silently. On a phone: left side moves, right side looks.
👂The creatures are blind and hunt sound. Scanning one wakes it up. A clap wakes everything nearby.
💧The exit drips. Follow the green.

Headphones help: you can hear which side things are on.
