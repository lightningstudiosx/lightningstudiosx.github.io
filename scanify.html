📷 shoot the object from all around

put the object on a table with a plain background (a sheet of paper behind it is perfect). walk in a circle around it, taking evenly spaced photos — same height, same distance, object in the middle of the frame, every time. go counter-clockwise (looking down from above). 16 is good. 24–32 is better.

✂️ cut the object out of every photo

red = background (gone). start with auto-cut, then fix mistakes with the wand or brush. do it for every photo — the ✔ shows which are done.

wand: click a color to remove it (shift+click to add it back). brush paints object back in, eraser removes.

🪨 carve the block

every silhouette slices away the space outside it, from its own angle. what survives all of them is your object.

if the result looks mirrored or like a blob, flip the walked direction and carve again.

🧊 your model

drag to spin, scroll to zoom. open the .ply in Blender / MeshLab / Windows 3D Viewer. dents and holes can't be carved from silhouettes — it's the object's outline from every angle, wearing your photos.
