The Cosmic Calendar

Squeeze all 13.8 billion years of history into one calendar year. The Big Bang happens at midnight on January 1. Right now is the very last stroke of midnight on December 31. Every day has its event. Tap any day to see it hour by hour.

Dates are scientists' best estimates, and ones marked "maybe" are still argued about. Times before Earth come from the standard model of the expanding universe (ΛCDM with Planck 2018 numbers). Geological colors are the official ones from the International Commission on Stratigraphy. Made for kai.fun.
