# ADVENTURE. - credits

Every photographed sky, surface and household object in the game comes from **Poly Haven** (polyhaven.com) and is released under **CC0 1.0**
(public domain). Downloaded originals are in `assets_raw/` (fetched by `tools/fetch_assets.py`); `tools/optimise_assets.py` and
`tools/optimise_tex.py` turn them into the files in `assets/` (one .glb per model with 512 px-1k JPEG textures, decimated where noted;
1k HDRIs for lighting plus a 3072 px JPEG of the upper half of each sky as the visible backdrop). Total `assets/` size: 14.7 MB.

Changes: pool tiles and carpet textures are desaturated so the game can tint them (pale aqua tiles, blue carpet). The laptop is scaled to 55%,
the table to 1.0 m across. Fan decimated to 8k triangles, shell to 3k, alarm clock to 6k.

## Built in code (no external asset)

The endless terrain and far hills, instanced grass and wildflowers, the door, floor lamp, pool, slide, swing set, staircase, traffic light and road,
power pylons and wires, toy track, and the six small items (cassette, key, marble, floppy disk, toy car; the seashell is a Poly Haven scan).
All sound is synthesised live with WebAudio: no samples.
