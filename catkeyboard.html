Cat on a
Keyboard

Your human works too much. Time to fix that, one paw at a time.

⬅️Arrows / WASD walk. Normal walking sometimes presses the keys under your paws.
🐾Space taps the key in the dashed box in front of your nose. Walk until the box is on the glowing key, then tap.
🤫Hold Shift to tiptoe: a bit slower, and completely silent.
👀Never press keys while your human is looking at the screen.
⌫When they look back, too much gibberish gets deleted. Clean up with Backspace.
⏎Spell the message, then step on Enter to send it.
