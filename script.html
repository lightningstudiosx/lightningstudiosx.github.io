How it works

Nine dots

Every glyph sits on the same 3×3 grid of dots, numbered 0–8 from the top left. A letter is just a few lines between dots — k is the top line, the middle line down, and the bottom line.

Extra dots and one ring

Some letters add a little dot on a grid point: a is a bar with a dot above, e the same bar with a dot below. o is the only round letter — a ring around the middle.

Numbers are dots

A digit is that many dots on the grid with a line underneath (so you can tell it from a letter). Zero is a tiny ring.

The 17 letters

Five vowels (yellow) and twelve consonants. Each letter always makes exactly one sound.

Trace practice

Drag from dot to dot to draw each stroke. Tap a dot to add a little dot. For o, draw a circle around the middle. Order and direction don’t matter.
