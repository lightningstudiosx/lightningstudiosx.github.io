    Build a Roblox Game

    One giant world of blocks that everybody builds together, live. Pick any colour and build anywhere: on the big wall, on the baseplate, on top of other blocks, even out on the grass. Every block is shared with everyone on this page and with everyone playing the Roblox game.

    The game never stays the same: the wall is built live by everyone, so after launch it keeps changing every minute.

    Hop in to walk around as a block. Roblox players see you as a moving block with a chat sign, and you see them as blocky figures.

    Tools: place (1), paint (2), remove (3); click / tap where the ghost block shows. Look: drag to look all the way round, right-drag or two fingers to slide, wheel / pinch to zoom, WASD + Q / E (or the stick) to fly. The map (bottom right) jumps you anywhere. Player: WASD or the stick to walk, Space to jump, Enter to chat.
