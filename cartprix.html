How to race

The list. Every race hands you a grocery list. Items sit on specific shelves, marked with a yellow price tag and a red ring on the floor. Drive close to the shelf to grab one. Plan your route: the order is up to you.

The wobble. Your front wheel pulls to one side, harder the faster you go. Counter-steer. Every cart pulls differently.

Kick & ride. Running fills the kick meter. Hold kick to push off and ride the back bar: faster, slippier, and it drifts. Kick as a footstep lands (the light turns green) for a PERFECT kick. Drifting refills the meter.

Checkout. With the full list, drive into any checkout lane. First to pay wins. Top three take a medal.

SALE bins. Rotisserie chicken (boost), free sample (shield), clean-up on aisle 5 (drop a spill), price check (freezes the leader).
