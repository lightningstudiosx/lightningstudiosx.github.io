FAQ

Is this a real sandwich?

No. Every crumb hole, lettuce vein, tomato seed and ham fold is generated by code when the page loads. There are no photos and no 3D scans.

How do I look inside?

Press Inside to slice it in half, or Apart to lift every layer off. Click the glowing dots (or the sandwich itself) to read about each layer.

Why is the lettuce under the tomato?

A leaf of lettuce keeps the juicy tomato from making the bread soggy. It's a real sandwich trick.

Can I eat it?

Not on this screen. But "Download me" gives you the whole thing as a 3D file you can open in Blender or put in a game.

What happens if I spin it a lot?

Crumbs fall off. We count them.
